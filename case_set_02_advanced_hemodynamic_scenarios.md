# Simulated case set: Advanced hemodynamic reasoning

Entirely synthetic independent premedical study material. Revised 27 October 2026. The author confirmed that scenarios are independent of real encounters/charts. This reconstruction preserves the earlier topic coverage while withdrawing unsupported medication doses, device adjustments and automatic escalation thresholds. Earlier versions remain in Git history.

## How to use these worked examples

Read the paired patient examples and integrated clinical narratives linked under each scenario. The examples explain interpretation and conditional care decisions; no student answers or scoring are required. See the [procedure-organized CTICU casebook](CTICU_PROCEDURE_EXAMPLES.md) and [source guide](references/bibliography.md).

## Scenario 1: Shock immediately after CABG

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-02-01). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-02-01).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult after coronary bypass has hypotension, low estimated output, hypothermia and ongoing drain output.

**Worked example interpretation**

Use surgical assessment and verified output measurements to distinguish blood loss from pump failure, while allowing coexistence. A vasopressor, inotrope or transfusion discussion addresses different mechanisms; none is selected solely by MAP. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** A single cardiac-index or drain-output cutoff is not a complete treatment rule.

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult on VA-ECMO has improving lactate but oliguria and concern about limb perfusion.

**Worked example interpretation**

Falling lactate does not settle limb perfusion or kidney questions; evaluate each organ and circuit configuration. Anticoagulation, vasoactive and analgesic choices require bleeding/perfusion context and specialist oversight. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** Post-oxygenator oxygenation does not alone establish upper-body oxygenation. Flow changes, cannula procedures and renal-support prescriptions require an ECMO team.

## Scenario 6: Lung transplantation and graft dysfunction

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-02-06). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-02-06).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional lung-transplant recipient develops poor compliance, impaired gas exchange and fluid accumulation.

**Worked example interpretation**

Check transplant timing, imaging and competing causes before using PGD classification; donor/recipient size and mechanics need context. Diuresis, ventilator support and immune therapy answer different questions; none is established by poor compliance alone. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.
