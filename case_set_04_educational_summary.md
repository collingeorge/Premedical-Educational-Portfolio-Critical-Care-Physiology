# Case Set 04: Educational Summary and Synthesis

**Number of Scenarios:** 13

---

## Overview of Scenario Set

This educational summary synthesizes key learning themes across 13 postoperative cardiothoracic critical care scenarios.

---

## Cross-Scenario Educational Themes

- No codes, no emergent returns to OR.
- Hemodynamics stable in majority; several scenarios required uptitration of norepinephrine.
- Multiple evolving AKI cases; none met criteria for CRRT.
- Two active infectious evaluations (POD5 fever, POD4 low-grade fever).
- Four scenarios flagged for early inotrope consideration.
- Respiratory status largely unchanged; several HFNC-dependent scenarios.
- Delirium present in expected postoperative MAZE/elderly scenarios.

---

## Scenario-by-Scenario Synopsis

### Scenario 1 — POD0 CABG ×3 | Vasoplegic Shock

**Scenario progression:** MAP 58–62 on norepinephrine + vasopressin; lactate 3.4 stable; gradual rewarming.
**Support:** NE 0.10, vaso 0.03; intubated, ventilator stable.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Continue rewarming; trend lactate.
- Assess readiness to down-titrate norepinephrine once MAP >65.

---

### Scenario 2 — POD2 Redo AVR | Low CO + AKI

**Scenario progression:** CI 1.9–2.2; lactate 2.7 rising; Cr 1.7; mild pulmonary edema.
**Interventions:** Gentle diuresis; SBT deferred.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Consider milrinone if CI <2.2.
- Echo if lactate rises.
- Trend Cr, UOP.

---

### Scenario 3 — POD4 MV Repair + MAZE | Sinus Bradycardia

**Scenario progression:** HR 55–62 limiting CO; MAP stable on NE 0.02–0.04; intermittent delirium.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Evaluate pacing threshold + need.
- Diurese only if stable.
- CAM-ICU optimization; promote mobilization.

---

### Scenario 4 — POD3 CABG + TVR | Moderate RV Dysfunction

**Scenario progression:** HFNC dependent; CVP ~12; mild AKI.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Cautious diuresis; avoid PEEP escalation.
- Afterload optimization.
- Repeat echo if hypotension or rising lactate.

---

### Scenario 5 — POD2 Redo Mechanical AVR + Graft

**Scenario progression:** INR 2.1; MAP maintained on NE 0.02–0.04; no bleeding.
**Discussion priorities:**
- INR goal 2–3; confirm level.
- Assess for pressor wean.
- Consider SBT if hemodynamically stable.

---

### Scenario 6 — POD1 CABG + Bio-MVR | Biventricular Dysfunction

**Scenario progression:** CI 2.0–2.2; FiO₂ 45–50%; Cr stable.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Consider inotrope if lactate increases.
- Cautious diuresis.
- POCUS for filling pressures.

---

### Scenario 7 — POD3 CABG + MAZE | Bradycardia + Delirium

**Scenario progression:** HR 58–65; mild AKI; episodic delirium.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Assess pacing candidacy.
- Mobilization, normalize sleep–wake cycle.
- Consider NE wean if tolerated.

---

### Scenario 8 — POD1 CABG + LV Aneurysmectomy | Severe Low CO Syndrome

**Scenario progression:** CI 1.8–2.1; lactate 3.8; FiO₂ 50%; Cr rising.
**Discussion priorities (High Priority):**
- Discuss milrinone initiation.
- If deterioration: early MCS discussion (IABP/Impella).
- Reassess preload/afterload with echo.

---

### Scenario 9 — POD5 CABG + MVR | New Fever

**Scenario progression:** Temp 38.5; WBC 15.2; cultures drawn; empiric vanc + Zosyn started.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Review cultures.
- CXR.
- Re-evaluate antibiotics based on PCT + hemodynamics.
- Consider ID consult if cultures positive.

---

### Scenario 10 — POD2 CABG + TVR | RV Dysfunction

**Scenario progression:** HFNC dependent; RV borderline; MAP adequate.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Gentle diuresis.
- Attempt oxygen weaning if tolerated.
- Avoid PEEP increases.

---

### Scenario 11 — POD1 Bentall + Hemiarch | Dual Pressors

**Scenario progression:** MAP stable; lactate 2.5→1.9; INR 2.6; Cr slightly increased.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Repeat INR; reassess anticoag strategy.
- Cautious diuresis.
- Reassess with echo if hypotensive.
- Monitor for bleeding (chest tube output, Hgb).

---

### Scenario 12 — POD4 CABG + AVR | Persistent Volume Overload

**Scenario progression:** Diuretic resistant; borderline AKI; HFNC stable; low-grade fever.
**Discussion priorities:**
- Evaluate sequential diuretics (metolazone + loop).
- Review infectious workup and procalcitonin.
- Ensure MAP adequacy before escalating diuresis.

---

### Scenario 13 — POD2 CABG + MV Repair | Rising Lactate

**Scenario progression:** Lactate 2.8→3.2; borderline CI; HFNC dependent; AKI evolving.
**Discussion priorities (High Priority):**
- Early inotrope discussion.
- Strict lactate/UOP trend.
- Echo today.
- Avoid SBT.

## Educational Discussion Priorities

### 1. Hemodynamics

- Reassess pressor requirements for all scenarios during discussion.
- Inotrope candidates: Scenarios 2, 6, 8, 13.
- Pressor weaning opportunities: 5, 7, 11.

### 2. Renal Function / AKI Monitoring

**Active AKI or evolving AKI in:** 2, 6, 8, 11, 12, 13

**Actions:**
- Trend Cr/UOP throughout scenario progression.
- Consider early nephrology discussion for 2, 8, 12, 13.
- Avoid over-diuresis in borderline MAP scenarios.

### 3. Respiratory Management

- SBT candidates: 5, 6 pending hemodynamic stability.
- HFNC weaning: 4, 9, 10, 12 if SpO₂ >92% without increased work of breathing.
- Avoid high PEEP in RV dysfunction (4, 7, 10).

### 4. Infection Control

- Culture review: Scenarios 9, 12.
- Recheck procalcitonin.
- Chest X-rays for fever eval vs pulmonary edema.
- Adjust antibiotics per sensitivities and clinical course.

### 5. Delirium / Mobility

- Mobilization priority: 3, 7, 12.
- Continue non-pharmacologic delirium strategies.
- Optimize sleep hygiene across scenario set.

### 6. Echocardiography

**Recommended reassessment:** 2, 4, 8, 13

Focus: CO, filling pressures, RV function, effusion/tamponade screen.

## Key Educational Questions

### Laboratory & Diagnostic Actions

- Confirm all cultures obtained.
- Labs: BMP, CBC, INR (valve scenarios), lactate, PCT.
- Serial lactates for Scenarios 1, 2, 8, 13.

### Clinical Tasks

- Assess diuresis response.
- Evaluate SBT readiness where applicable.
- POCUS for volume assessment on multiple scenarios.

### Anticoagulation

- Review INR for Mechanical Valve Scenarios: 5, 11.
- Consider heparin bridging if INR subtherapeutic.

### Consultations

- Cardiology/CT Surgery: Scenarios 8, 13 (low CO syndrome).
- Nephrology: Scenarios 2, 8, 12, 13 if AKI worsens.
- Infectious Disease: If cultures positive or source unclear (9, 12).
