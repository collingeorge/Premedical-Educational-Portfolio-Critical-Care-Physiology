# Respiratory failure: study objectives

Independent premedical study objectives, revised 27 October 2026. These concern conceptual interpretation rather than initiating or adjusting ventilation.

## Gas exchange and mechanics

- Distinguish oxygenation, ventilation and systemic oxygen delivery.
- Explain shunt, dead space and ventilation–perfusion mismatch without assigning one mechanism from a single blood gas.
- Calculate P:F using FiO2 as a fraction; explain why FiO2 alone cannot establish the ratio or an ARDS severity category.
- Distinguish predicted from actual body weight in lung-protection discussions.
- Interpret plateau pressure, total PEEP and driving pressure only with their measurement conditions, effort and chest-wall context.
- Explain why volume control, pressure control and adaptive modes are not interchangeable or independently safe.

## Evidence appraisal

Use the ATS and ELSO sources in the [source guide](../references/bibliography.md). Separate strong from conditional recommendations, identify the studied population and distinguish a mortality outcome from a change in oxygenation. Do not convert one ratio, pressure or pH into an automatic proning, paralysis or ECMO trigger.

## Airway and support context

Identify whether the fictional patient is intubated, on NIV, on HFNC or receiving conventional oxygen. An extubation spontaneous breathing trial concerns liberation from invasive support; it should not be prescribed for a vignette that is already extubated on HFNC. Explain that oxygen-delivery and ventilatory-support devices are not equivalent.

For each case, describe alternative causes of deterioration, missing measurements and the information needed for expert reassessment. No ventilator prescription, intubation plan or device procedure is supplied.
