# Mechanical ventilation: educational concepts

> Independent educational work. Not a clinical protocol, prescription, patient handout, or validated decision aid. This revision is a draft pending clinical and publication review.

## Measurement and scope

Lower tidal-volume strategies in ARDS are expressed per **predicted body weight**, a height-based estimate, not actual body weight. Plateau pressure is interpreted during appropriate no-flow measurement conditions, and patient effort and chest-wall mechanics can affect its meaning. Static compliance is tidal volume divided by plateau pressure minus total PEEP under suitable passive conditions. Auto-PEEP matters.

Driving pressure is associated with outcome in ARDS, but this does not validate a universal target below 12 or 14 cm H2O for every patient. Oxygen and carbon dioxide goals are likewise context-dependent; values from an ARDS trial are not general targets for all ICU disease states.

## Modes and interaction

Volume control and adaptive pressure modes such as PRVC are distinct. A set or targeted volume does not guarantee delivery under leaks, pressure limits, or changing effort. Pressure control limits the set inspiratory pressure but does not guarantee lung protection or a fixed tidal volume. Mode names alone do not establish safety.

PEEP can recruit lung or overdistend it, with different effects on gas exchange and RV loading. Avoid blanket “low PEEP” rules in RV dysfunction; both derecruitment and overdistension can increase RV stress. See [physiology notes](icu_physiology_notes.md).

## Evidence interpretation

ATS 2024 conditionally supports higher PEEP without recruitment maneuvers for moderate-to-severe ARDS and recommends against prolonged recruitment maneuvers. Neuromuscular blockade and ECMO recommendations concern selected populations and are conditional; neither is an automatic first-line treatment for every low oxygenation ratio.

Liberation from ventilation involves assessment of the underlying illness, respiratory reserve, airway protection, secretions, and the planned post-extubation support. A fixed PEEP, oxygen fraction, respiratory rate, or recovery score does not independently determine readiness. This note provides no ventilator settings, intubation protocol, or rescue algorithm.

## Sources and applicability

- [SCCM/ESICM 2026 adult sepsis guideline](https://sccm.org/survivingsepsiscampaign/guidelines-and-resources/surviving-sepsis-campaign-adult-guidelines): the current guideline supersedes the 2021 edition. For ongoing fluid decisions, dynamic responsiveness assessment is preferable to isolated filling pressures.
- [ATS 2024 ARDS guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10870893/): conditionally favors higher PEEP without recruitment maneuvers in moderate-to-severe ARDS and recommends against prolonged recruitment maneuvers.
- [ATS/ESICM/SCCM 2017 ventilation guideline](https://academic.oup.com/ajrccm/article/195/9/1253/8500154): lower tidal volumes use predicted body weight, not actual body weight.

These summaries are educational and do not reproduce the full guideline, its exceptions, or its implementation requirements.
