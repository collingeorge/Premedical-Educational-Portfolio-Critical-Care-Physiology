Entirely synthetic independent premedical study material. Revised 27 October 2026. The author confirmed that scenarios are independent of real encounters/charts. This reconstruction preserves the earlier topic coverage while withdrawing unsupported medication doses, device adjustments and automatic escalation thresholds. ## How to use these worked examples

Read the paired patient examples and integrated clinical narratives linked under each scenario. The examples explain interpretation and conditional care decisions; no student answers or scoring are required. ## Scenario 1: Postoperative hemodynamic instability

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-01). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-01).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional postoperative adult develops hypotension, rising lactate and chest-drain output. Cardiac function and the bleeding source have not been established.

**Worked example interpretation**

Separate blood loss, pump failure, obstruction and vascular tone; focused imaging and serial perfusion findings discriminate the branches. Vasoactive support does not correct a surgical bleeding source; analgesic/sedative effects also belong in the medication review. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** Tamponade is an obstructive mechanism. A drain-output number alone does not establish a re-exploration threshold; postoperative tamponade should not be reduced to an automatic pericardiocentesis instruction.

## Scenario 2: DNI status and respiratory distress

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-02). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-02).

**Synthetic vignette:** An older fictional adult with COPD and pneumonia is distressed and has difficulty tolerating non-invasive ventilation. DNR/DNI is documented; other treatment preferences are unknown.

**Worked example interpretation**

Clarify the treatment ceiling and trial goals before interpreting NIV intolerance; distinguish reversible distress from a separately chosen symptom-only plan. Analgesia and anxiolysis require respiratory reassessment; a sedative combination is not implied by DNI. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** DNR/DNI does not itself mean comfort-only care or justify accepting hypoxemia or ignoring respiratory depression. Palliative support may accompany active treatment.

## Scenario 3: Worsening septic shock

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-03). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-03).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult with suspected infection has increasing vasopressor requirements, mottling, oliguria and rising lactate.

**Worked example interpretation**

Reassess infection/source control and perfusion while asking whether additional fluid is both effective and tolerated. Antimicrobials and vasopressor support are distinct interventions; culture/resistance and organ function inform drug discussions. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** A lactate trend is not specific for tissue hypoxia. The current sepsis guideline is not interchangeable with an unconditional three-drug escalation rule.

## Scenario 4: Rib fractures and impaired ventilation

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional trauma patient has chest-wall pain, shallow breathing and increasing oxygen needs. Associated injuries are incompletely described.

**Worked example interpretation**

Evaluate chest injury, ventilation and analgesic adequacy together; a pain score alone cannot exclude worsening respiratory injury. Local/regional or systemic analgesia are discussion options after injury and bleeding-risk assessment; respiratory effects matter. **Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult with suspected fulminant myocarditis has poor perfusion despite initial support. Ventricular function, rhythm and the cause of inflammation need reassessment.

**Worked example interpretation**

Characterize rhythm and ventricular involvement before comparing pharmacologic support with expert mechanical-support assessment. Inotrope, vasopressor and sedation choices depend on the assessed physiology; suspected myocarditis does not select an empiric immune-treatment regimen. ## Scenario 6: Pancreatitis with respiratory and renal dysfunction

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-06). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-06).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional postoperative adult with pancreatitis has pain, positive fluid balance, worsening breathing and rising creatinine.

**Worked example interpretation**

The WATERFALL result argues against assuming more fluid is better; established organ failure differs from its trial population and requires individualized reassessment. Analgesic choice and renal/hepatic exposure need review; no fixed high-volume fluid or drug infusion follows from the diagnosis. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** Routine aggressive fluids, a fixed infusion rate, automatic calcium replacement and a universal epidural preference are not established by this scenario.

## Scenario 7: Brain injury with aspiration and airway decline

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-07). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-07).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult with traumatic brain injury develops reduced alertness and suspected aspiration.

**Worked example interpretation**

Separate airway/oxygenation threats, injury progression and medication effects; a sedative-adjusted examination is not equivalent to exclusion of intracranial deterioration. Hypnotics, analgesics and osmotic agents have different purposes; no dose or ventilation target can be selected from the abbreviated case. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** Traumatic-brain-injury guidance is condition-specific. A CPP target cannot be transferred automatically to every neurologic disorder.

## Scenario 8: Sepsis with ARDS and kidney injury

[Paired synthetic application examples](APPLIED_CASES.md#icu-01-08). [Integrated workup and test reasoning](ICU_CTICU_WORKUP_CASES.md#icu-01-08).

**Synthetic vignette:** A fictional adult with pneumonia has bilateral opacities, impaired gas exchange, positive fluid balance and kidney dysfunction.

**Worked example interpretation**

Integrate lung-protection assessment, infection/source control and fluid tolerance rather than treating isolated oxygenation or creatinine values. Sedation or neuromuscular-block discussions depend on the actual ventilation problem; renal support is a separate indication-based decision. The linked paired example and integrated narrative explain the patient-specific context, investigation purposes and reassessment.

**Interpretation boundary:** FiO2 alone does not give a P:F ratio. P:F below 150 is not identical to the Berlin severe category. Neuromuscular blockade and renal support are not automatic responses to a single number.
