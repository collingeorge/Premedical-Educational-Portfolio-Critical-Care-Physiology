# Shock physiology: educational interpretation

> Independent educational work. Not a clinical protocol, prescription, patient handout, or validated decision aid. This revision is a draft pending clinical and publication review.

Shock is inadequate tissue perfusion or oxygen utilization in context; a blood pressure cutoff alone is insufficient. Mechanisms can coexist and change over time.

| Mechanism | Educational features and caveats |
|---|---|
| Hypovolemic | Reduced effective circulating volume may limit flow. Low CVP or a small IVC alone does not establish fluid need. |
| Distributive | Altered vascular tone and microcirculatory dysfunction can occur with apparently preserved output; myocardial dysfunction may coexist. |
| Cardiogenic | Cardiac dysfunction can limit forward flow, often with congestion. Preserved EF does not exclude important cardiac pathology. |
| Obstructive | Mechanical impairment of filling or outflow can limit circulation; interpretation depends on the cause. |
| Mixed | More than one mechanism is common, particularly after surgery and in complex critical illness. |

Assessment integrates history, examination, serial perfusion observations, appropriately interpreted imaging, and relevant measurements. Lactate is influenced by production and clearance and is not specific for tissue hypoxia. ScvO2 and SvO2 cannot be interchanged or interpreted as universal treatment thresholds.

Static filling pressures are not sufficient to choose fluids. Dynamic tests, when valid in the clinical context, address responsiveness; they do not independently establish fluid tolerance or indicate treatment. The current sepsis guideline and its patient-specific qualifications should be consulted rather than treating an older summary as a protocol.

No drug sequence, resuscitation dose, or device escalation rule is supplied here. The synthetic scenarios are study exercises, not clinical instructions.

## Sources and applicability

- [SCCM/ESICM 2026 adult sepsis guideline](https://sccm.org/survivingsepsiscampaign/guidelines-and-resources/surviving-sepsis-campaign-adult-guidelines): the current guideline supersedes the 2021 edition. For ongoing fluid decisions, dynamic responsiveness assessment is preferable to isolated filling pressures.
- [ATS 2024 ARDS guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10870893/): conditionally favors higher PEEP without recruitment maneuvers in moderate-to-severe ARDS and recommends against prolonged recruitment maneuvers.
- [ATS/ESICM/SCCM 2017 ventilation guideline](https://academic.oup.com/ajrccm/article/195/9/1253/8500154): lower tidal volumes use predicted body weight, not actual body weight.

These summaries are educational and do not reproduce the full guideline, its exceptions, or its implementation requirements.
